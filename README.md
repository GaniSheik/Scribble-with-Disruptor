# Scribble-with-Disruptor
Scribble.rs is a browser-based Pictionary-style game. One player draws a chosen word while the others guess in chat, and faster guesses score more.  In your fork, each turn a random non-drawer becomes the Disruptor. They can’t guess, but they scribble on the canvas and earn points. Their pen is shaky, flips, lags, and changes brush and colors.
